# FAQ

**Do you provide keys or games?** No. Bring your own `prod.keys` and your own dumps.

**Is the source available?** Not until v1.0. This repo is the public kit.

**The console is not reachable.** Open SSI (it must be running), check the IP and ports 2000/2003, wake the console if it slept.

**Does it support the Switch 2?** SSM reads Switch 2 games from NUP archives (and from NSP or XCI) and lists them next to your other games, with a Switch / Switch 2 filter. It cannot send them to a Switch 2 yet: SSI has no Switch 2 version.

**Can I make my own theme?** Yes, see [themes/](../themes/README.md).
